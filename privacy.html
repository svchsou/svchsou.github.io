Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to Local Privacy

Strataform ("we", "our", or "us") is designed as a focused, distraction-free spatial strategy and mindfulness application. We believe that executive tools must treat personal data with absolute sanctity. Strataform does not require user accounts, registration, email sign-ups, or social logins.

2. On-Device Cognitive Telemetry

All gameplay analytics—including Focus Scores, Agitation Indices, Cadence Variance, puzzle solution paths, and personal completion milestones—are calculated and stored 100% locally on your physical device.

  • No touch coordinates, velocity vectors, or cognitive indicators are ever transmitted to any external server.
  • Your gameplay data remains completely private within your operating system's secure local sandbox.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To support ongoing puzzle creation and maintain complimentary access to daily challenges, Strataform integrates Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or IDFA), general device specifications, and crash diagnostics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads and combat fraud.
  • Players can permanently remove all advertisements via the in-app purchase "Strataform Lifetime".
  • For more information on how Google processes data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit & Google Play Billing)

All digital purchases (such as "Strataform Lifetime") are handled exclusively by Apple App Store (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information. Transaction receipts are verified cryptographically on-device with the respective platform.

5. Children's Privacy

Strataform is built for general audiences and mindful adults. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.
